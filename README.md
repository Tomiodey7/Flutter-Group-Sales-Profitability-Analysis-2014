### 📌 Business Problem
Flutter Group needed an end-to-end review of sales and profitability for 2014 to present at the senior executives' summit. 
The goal was to answer: Who are top performers? Which regions/cities drive revenue? What products & payment methods dominate?

### 📂 Dataset
- **Period:** Full Year 2014 + Q1 2015
- **Metrics:** Salesperson, Product Category, Product Name, Quantity, Revenue, City, State, Region, Shipping City, Payment Method
- **Key KPIs Tracked:** Total Revenue, Revenue by Region/City/State, Salesperson Performance, Top Products, Monthly Trend, Payment Method

### 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used
- **Data Cleaning & Analysis:** Excel
- **Dashboarding & Visual Storytelling:** Power BI / Canva-style Reporting
- **Analysis Performed:** KPI extraction, ranking, trend analysis, distribution analysis, category breakdown

### 📈 Key Insights & Results

**1. Performance KPIs:**
- **Top Salesperson:** Nancy - $104K+ revenue
- **Best Performing Region:** New York - $67K+
- **Peak Sales Month:** December - $66K+ (Range Peak)

**2. Geography Analysis:**
- **Top Cities by Revenue:** New York topped with $67,000, followed by Chicago at $44,000
- **Revenue by Region:** North 20.68% ($91.2K), East 24.36%, South 21.57%, West 33.29% ($146.2K) - West leads
- **Top States:** NY generated highest revenue at $67K, followed by OR, FL, TN, IL
- **Shipping:** New York shipped most revenue $67.18K, Las Vegas least $15.37K

**3. Product Insights:**
- **Product Category by Revenue:** Beverages topped by quantity purchased, followed by Dried Fruits & Baked Goods
- **Top 6 Products by Quantity:**
    - Chocolate - 1,399
    - Clam Chowder - 1,744
    - Coffee - 1,641
    - Crab Meat - 1,584
    - Curry Sauce - 1,725
    - Green Tea - 1,589
    - **Total:** 9,482 units

**4. Operational Insights:**
- **Monthly Trend:** December best at $67K, June & October $56K+, February lowest $20K - strong holiday seasonality
- **Most Used Payment Method:** Credit Card (73% - 101 orders), Check 12.7%, Cash only 34 orders (least used)
- **Top Customer:** Company D ranked first with $8.8K revenue

**5. Sales Team Performance:**
- Nancy led with $104K, Anne second $94K, Andrew $67K, Mariya $45K
